What Is Saju? A Complete Guide

Saju (사주, 四柱推命 in Chinese characters, pronounced "sa-ju") literally means "Four Pillars" — the four columns of your destiny chart built from the year, month, day, and hour of your birth. Each pillar consists of two characters: a Heavenly Stem (천간, 天干) and an Earthly Branch (지지, 地支), producing eight characters in total (팔자, 八字).

This 3,000-year-old system originated in Tang Dynasty China and was refined over centuries by Korean scholars into one of East Asia's most respected and detailed forms of life guidance. Today it is practised by millions across Korea, China, Japan, and the wider Korean diaspora.

The Five Elements in Saju

Each of the 10 Heavenly Stems and 12 Earthly Branches is assigned one of the Five Elements: Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), Water (水). The balance — or imbalance — of these elements in your chart determines your elemental constitution, personality strengths, health tendencies, and the types of fortune cycles you will experience.

Daewoon — Your 10-Year Major Fortune Cycles

One of Saju's most powerful features is the calculation of Daewoon (대운, 大運) — 10-year major fortune periods that shift the overall quality of your destiny at regular intervals. Knowing when your Daewoon changes — and what element governs each period — allows you to plan major life decisions (career moves, marriage, investments) with greater wisdom.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny)?

Saju is a Korean-Chinese astrology system using the year, month, day, and hour of birth to build an 8-character destiny chart. It analyses personality, life cycles, compatibility, career, and health through the Five Elements.

How is Saju different from Western astrology?

Western astrology uses planetary positions in the zodiac. Saju uses the Five Elements and the 10-year cycle system (Daewoon) — placing equal importance on birth time as a fourth pillar.

Do I need my birth time?

Yes, ideally. The birth hour is the fourth pillar. Without it, the chart is incomplete. Code Destiny accepts partial birth data but recommends including the exact hour for a full reading.
