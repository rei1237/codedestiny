What Is Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology)?

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數) — translated as "Purple Star Astrology" or "Emperor Star Astrology" — is one of the two great pillars of Chinese metaphysics (the other being BaZi/Saju). Developed during the Northern Song Dynasty and attributed to the Taoist sage Chen Tuan (陳摶), it has been refined over a thousand years into an extraordinarily detailed life-reading system.

The system places over 130 stars into a 12-palace grid based on your birth year, month, day, and hour. The relative positions of the emperor star Zi Wei (紫微星), the general star Tian Fu (天府), and dozens of supporting and auxiliary stars create a unique cosmic signature that maps every major area of your life.

The 12 Palaces of Zi Wei Dou Shu

  • Life Palace (命宮) — Core personality and overall life theme
  • Siblings Palace (兄弟宮) — Relationships with siblings and peers
  • Marriage Palace (夫妻宮) — Romantic relationships and marriage
  • Children Palace (子女宮) — Relationship with children and creative legacy
  • Wealth Palace (財帛宮) — Financial fortune and earning ability
  • Health Palace (疾厄宮) — Physical health and vitality
  • Travel Palace (遷移宮) — Overseas luck and life away from home
  • Friends Palace (僕役宮) — Social connections and employees
  • Career Palace (官祿宮) — Vocation, career trajectory, and authority
  • Property Palace (田宅宮) — Real estate, home life, and accumulation
  • Fortune Palace (福德宮) — Inner happiness, hobbies, spiritual life
  • Parents Palace (父母宮) — Relationship with parents and authority figures

How Zi Wei Dou Shu Differs from Western Astrology

Western astrology uses 10–15 celestial bodies in 12 zodiac signs. Zi Wei Dou Shu uses over 130 stars in 12 life-area palaces. Western astrology focuses heavily on psychological archetypes; Zi Wei Dou Shu is more fate-oriented and situational — mapping what is likely to occur in each domain of life rather than personality tendencies alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a 1,000-year-old Chinese astrology system using 130+ stars across 12 life palaces to map destiny in extraordinary detail. It requires your birth year, month, day, and hour to generate a complete chart.

How does Zi Wei differ from BaZi / Saju?

BaZi (Saju) analyses Five Element cycles and heavenly stem/earthly branch combinations. Zi Wei uses a fixed palace grid of star constellations. The two systems complement each other — many practitioners use both for a fuller reading.
