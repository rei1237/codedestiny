How to Get the Most from Your Tarot Reading

Tarot works best when you approach it with a clear, open mind. Before drawing your cards, take a moment to breathe deeply and formulate your question. The more focused your intention, the more meaningful the symbols that emerge will be.

Our AI interpretations draw on the Rider-Waite-Smith symbolic tradition, the most widely studied tarot system. Each card carries layered meanings related to the four elements (Fire, Water, Earth, Air), numerological principles, and archetypal psychology.

The Major Arcana — cards like The Fool, The High Priestess, The Tower, and The World — represent major life themes and turning points. The Minor Arcana's four suits (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles) reflect everyday situations in creativity, emotion, thought, and material life.
