What Is a Nakshatra?

The word nakshatra (नक्षत्र) comes from Sanskrit — naksha (approach, map) + tra (guard, protect). Ancient Vedic astronomers divided the sky into 27 equal sections of 13°20' each, tracing the path the Moon travels during its 27.3-day sidereal cycle. Each section is a nakshatra — a lunar mansion with its own ruling deity, symbol, planetary lord, and characteristic qualities.

In Vedic tradition, the Moon is the mind — and your birth nakshatra is the constellation that shaped your mind the moment you arrived in this world.

While Western astrology places greatest emphasis on the Sun sign, Jyotish astrologers regard the Moon sign (Rashi) and especially the birth nakshatra as your most fundamental astrological signature. Your Sun sign changes roughly every 30 days; the Moon changes nakshatra approximately every 24 hours — making it vastly more personal.

The Four Pada System — Quarter Divisions

Each nakshatra is further divided into four padas (quarters), each spanning 3°20'. These quarters correspond to the four aims of human life in Hindu philosophy: Dharma (purpose), Artha (wealth), Kama (pleasure), and Moksha (liberation). A person born with the Moon in the first pada of Rohini has a different flavour from one in the fourth pada, even though both are "Rohini" nakshatras.

Vimshottari Dasha — Your Life Timing System

Your birth nakshatra determines your Vimshottari dasha starting point — the planetary cycle system that maps the timing of major life events. The full dasha cycle spans 120 years, divided into nine planetary periods:

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a nakshatra?

A nakshatra is one of 27 lunar mansions in Vedic astrology — the section of sky where the Moon is located at your birth. It is considered the most personal and precise indicator of your personality and destiny.

How do I find my nakshatra?

Is nakshatra the same as moon sign?

No. Your moon sign (Rashi) is which of 12 zodiac signs the Moon is in. Your nakshatra is a finer sub-division — one of 27 sections, each containing 13°20' of the 360° zodiac.
