What Is Saju?

Saju (사주, 四柱推命) literally means "Four Pillars Fate Calculation" in Korean. The system builds a destiny chart from four key moments surrounding your birth:

  • The Year you were born
  • The Month you were born
  • The Day you were born
  • The Hour you were born

Each of these four moments — or "pillars" (柱, ju) — is expressed as a pair of characters from the traditional Chinese sexagenary calendar: a Heavenly Stem (천간, 天干) and an Earthly Branch (지지, 地支). Four pillars × two characters each = eight characters total. This is why the system is also called 팔자 (八字, "Eight Characters") or, in Chinese, BaZi.

Your eight characters are a cosmic fingerprint — no two people born at different times share an identical chart.

A Brief History of Saju

The foundations of Saju trace back to the Han Dynasty (206 BCE – 220 CE) in China, when scholars began systematically correlating heavenly cycles with human destiny. The system evolved significantly during the Tang Dynasty (618–907 CE) under the influence of Li Xupai, and reached its mature, sophisticated form during the Song Dynasty (960–1279 CE) through the legendary master Xu Ziping — which is why the system is sometimes called Ziping BaZi in Chinese practice.

Saju entered Korea through the transmission of Chinese classical texts and was enthusiastically adopted by Korean scholars during the Joseon Dynasty (1392–1897). Over centuries, Korean practitioners developed their own interpretive traditions and refined the system for Korean cultural contexts. Today, Saju consultation before major life decisions — marriage, career moves, business launches, house purchases — is as normal in South Korea as horoscope column-reading is in the West.

The Five Elements — The Heart of Saju

Each heavenly stem and earthly branch carries one of the Five Elements: Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), and Water (水). These elements interact in two fundamental ways:

  • Generating Cycle (相生): Wood feeds Fire → Fire creates Earth (ash) → Earth contains Metal → Metal holds Water → Water nourishes Wood
  • Controlling Cycle (相剋): Wood penetrates Earth → Earth absorbs Water → Water extinguishes Fire → Fire melts Metal → Metal cuts Wood

The relative balance — or imbalance — of the five elements in your eight characters determines your elemental constitution. Someone with six Water characters and no Fire may experience emotional depth but lack passion and drive. A chart rich in Metal may indicate precision and discipline, but also rigidity.

The Day Master — Your Core Identity

In a Saju chart, the Day Master (일간, 日干) — the heavenly stem of your Day Pillar — is the most important single character. It represents your core self, your fundamental nature, and the lens through which all other chart elements are interpreted. Every other character in your chart is understood in relation to your Day Master.

For example, a person born with a Bing Fire (丙) Day Master is naturally radiant, enthusiastic, and inspiring — but may struggle with impulsiveness. A Ren Water (壬) Day Master is intellectually expansive and socially fluid, but can become scattered or evasive under pressure.

Daewoon — Your 10-Year Fate Cycles

One of Saju's most distinctive and practically useful features is the Daewoon (대운, 大運) system. Daewoon divides your life into sequential 10-year periods, each governed by a heavenly stem/earthly branch combination that shifts the overall quality and direction of your fortune.

Knowing your Daewoon schedule allows you to understand why some decades feel expansive and fortunate while others bring contraction and challenge — and to make life decisions accordingly. Major career moves, marriage, and business launches typically go best when aligned with a favourable Daewoon cycle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Saju" mean?

Saju (사주) means "Four Pillars" — the year, month, day, and hour pillars of your birth chart that together contain eight heavenly and earthly characters encoding your destiny.

Is Saju the same as BaZi?

Yes. Saju is the Korean name for the same system called BaZi (八字) in Chinese. Both mean the same thing and follow the same fundamental interpretive principles, with minor regional variations in emphasis.

How accurate is Saju?

Saju is highly accurate when your complete birth data is known, particularly the birth hour. Modern AI systems like Code Destiny apply consistent, multi-dimensional interpretation frameworks to generate readings equivalent to experienced human practitioners.
