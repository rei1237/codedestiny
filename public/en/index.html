Frequently Asked Questions

What is Saju (Four Pillars of Destiny)?

Saju (사주, 四柱推命) is a traditional Korean-Chinese fortune-telling system that analyses your destiny using the Four Pillars of your birth: Year, Month, Day, and Hour. Each pillar contains a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch, creating a unique chart of 8 characters (팔자, 八字). The chart reveals your elemental balance (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water), personality strengths, life challenges, and 10-year major fortune cycles (Daewoon, 大運).

How accurate is the Tarot reading?

Code Destiny's tarot readings use the complete 78-card Universal Waite deck with AI-generated interpretations tailored to your selected spread and question theme. Accuracy depends on how deeply you engage with the symbolism — tarot is a reflective tool for self-understanding, not a deterministic prediction.

What is Zi Wei Dou Shu?

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數, Purple Star Astrology) is one of the most sophisticated branches of Chinese metaphysics. It maps 12 life palaces — including wealth, relationships, career, health, and travel — using the positions of over 130 stars calculated from your birth date and time. It is widely regarded as more detailed than Western natal astrology.
