Three Approaches to Dream Interpretation

🎴 Dream Tarot

Major Arcana tarot cards are deeply connected to universal dream symbols. The Tower appearing in a dream may signal sudden change — echoing the Tower card's imagery of collapse and revelation. The Moon card and water dreams share themes of the unconscious and deception. Our Dream Tarot tool maps your dream images to their tarot equivalents and delivers a narrative reading drawn from both traditions.

🧠 Jungian Archetypal Analysis

Carl Jung believed dreams are the royal road to the unconscious — populated by universal archetypes including the Shadow, the Anima/Animus, the Wise Old Man, the Trickster, and the Self. Our Jungian dream analysis identifies these archetypes in your dream narratives and explores what aspects of your psyche are seeking integration.

💭 Freudian Dream Decoding

Sigmund Freud's dream theory held that dreams fulfil unconscious wishes, often expressed through displacement, condensation, and symbolism. Code Destiny's Freudian module interprets recurring symbols (teeth falling out, flying, being chased, water) through the lens of repressed desires and unresolved conflicts.

Common Dream Symbols Decoded

  • Flying — Desire for freedom; transcendence of limitations; spiritual aspiration
  • Falling — Loss of control; anxiety about failure; need for grounding
  • Teeth falling out — Anxiety about appearance; communication fears; loss of power
  • Water — The unconscious; emotions; creative flow; the feminine principle
  • Being chased — Avoiding a problem; confronting the Shadow; fear of consequences
  • Snake — Transformation and healing (Asclepius); deception; kundalini energy; primal instinct
  • Death — Transformation; ending of a life phase; the Tarot's Death card (transition, not literal death)
  • Fire — Passion; destruction and purification; the will; anger or inspiration
  • School or exam — Self-evaluation; performance anxiety; feeling unprepared for life's tests
  • House — The self; different rooms = different aspects of consciousness
