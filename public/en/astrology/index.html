What Is a Natal Birth Chart?

A natal chart (also called a birth chart or horoscope) is a map of the sky at the exact moment of your birth — showing where the Sun, Moon, and all eight planets were positioned relative to your birthplace. Astrologers read these placements, their arrangement in 12 houses, and the angles (aspects) between planets to understand personality, life themes, and timing.

Your birth chart is uniquely yours — like a cosmic fingerprint. No two people, even twins born minutes apart in the same room, have identical charts.

Key Components of Your Chart

The Big Three: Sun, Moon, Rising

Your Sun sign reflects your core identity and life purpose. Your Moon sign reveals your emotional inner world and intuitive responses. Your Rising sign (Ascendant) describes how you appear to others and the lens through which you experience life. Most people know their Sun sign — but understanding all three is the foundation of serious astrology.

The 12 Houses

The 12 houses divide the chart into areas of life: self/appearance, finances, communication, home, creativity, health, partnerships, shared resources, philosophy, career, social causes, and hidden matters. Planets in each house focus their energy on that life domain.

Major Aspects

Aspects are geometric angles between planets. Conjunctions (0°) blend energies. Trines (120°) create ease and talent. Squares (90°) generate tension and growth. Oppositions (180°) create awareness through contrast. Sextiles (60°) bring opportunity.
