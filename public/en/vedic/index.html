What Is Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)?

Vedic astrology (Jyotish, ज्योतिष) is the ancient Indian science of light and planetary influence, with roots in the sacred texts of the Vedas — among the oldest spiritual writings on Earth. Unlike Western astrology which uses the Tropical zodiac (based on seasons), Vedic astrology uses the Sidereal zodiac — calculated from the actual positions of stars in the sky.

This means your Vedic sun sign often differs from your Western sun sign by approximately 23 degrees — equivalent to about 23 days. Most people find their Vedic Moon sign (Rashi) and Ascendant (Lagna) more personally resonant than their Western Sun sign.

Key Components of Your Vedic Chart

Nakshatra — Your Lunar Mansion

The nakshatra (नक्षत्र) is one of 27 lunar mansions — the section of the sky the Moon occupies at birth. Each nakshatra is associated with a deity, symbol, ruling planet, and specific life themes. Your birth nakshatra is considered the most important indicator of your personality and life path in Vedic astrology.

Rashi — Your Moon Sign

Unlike Western astrology which emphasises the Sun sign, Vedic astrology places the Moon sign (Rashi) at the centre. Your Rashi reveals your emotional nature, instinctive responses, and the fundamental quality of your mind. It is the foundation of your Vedic chart.

Lagna — Your Ascendant

The Lagna (rising sign) is the zodiac sign on the eastern horizon at your birth moment. It shapes your physical appearance, approach to life, and the overall framing of your chart. In Vedic astrology, the Lagna lord (ruling planet) is the single most important planet in your chart.

Vimshottari Dasha — Life Cycle Periods

The Vimshottari dasha system divides life into planetary periods totalling 120 years. Each planet governs a major period (mahadasha) ranging from 6 to 20 years. Within each mahadasha, shorter sub-periods (antardasha) of each planet create a detailed timing map for life events — career peaks, relationship shifts, health changes, and spiritual openings.
